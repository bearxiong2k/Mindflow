# Visit comparison

## Intent

Help readers compare observations from repeat visits within
[Fieldbook's reading scope and current practice](../project.md).

## Present account

The [browsing summary](../evolve/browsing-summary.md) explains the accepted
recent-visits default and place filter. The
[walkthrough](../evidence/browsing-walkthrough.md) retains the limited evidence.
The [remaining question](../evolve/browsing-evidence.md#remaining-question) concerns
comparison after selecting a place; it does not reopen that default by itself.

## Current edge

Use conflicting observations from two visits to compare a combined reading
view with opening separate notes. Preserve the differences in evidence rather
than silently reconciling them for the reader. Choose the next method under
the project's current guidance; repeating the paper walkthrough is not required.

## Absorption boundary

Put accepted behavior in [the project account](../project.md) and useful new
reasoning in an Event discussion. Revise this direction around the remaining
edge; remove it if no continuing intent remains. A resolved local question
does not require preserving a completed steering log.
