# Pattern: Temporality

## Lens

Project meaning participates in time differently. Some meaning describes the
present, some guides change, some preserves evidence, some protects uncertainty,
some coordinates briefly, and some survives only as history. These are useful
possibilities rather than mandatory states.

Genuinely new or reforming meaning may need to expand before its durable
distinctions are known. As consequences stabilize, they may be absorbed into
more exact owners and formal realizations while the originating insight,
rationale, or unresolved remainder continues in its proper temporal role.

## Tension

Keeping everything current makes the system accumulate chronology and expired
coordination. Removing material too quickly can erase rationale, evidence, or a
choice that still matters. Stabilizing a statement can create useful authority;
leaving it provisional can preserve necessary openness.

Failure to absorb settled consequences leaves higher positions carrying
duplicate obligation. Premature absorption hides uncertainty and can formalize
the wrong interpretation. Lower-level evidence may justify reopening meaning,
but current behavior alone does not silently settle the upstream account.

Apparent overload may be temporary expansion that still belongs together, or a
settled collision of responsibilities that now need independent owners. Treat
that temporal difference as evidence rather than assuming either accumulation
or separation is permanent.

## Questions

- How should this meaning participate in the project now?
- What would make it become current, provisional, historical, or irrelevant?
- Has temporary material completed its work, and where did its meaning go?
- Which consequence has stabilized enough to need a more exact owner, and what
  insight or uncertainty should remain where it is?
- Has a settled consequence already been absorbed downstream while its
  provisional source still carries duplicate detail or obligation?
- Is the crowded owner still holding live uncertainty, or has distinct meaning
  stabilized enough to require independent ownership and change?
- What implementation, test, use, or historical evidence would justify
  reopening the upstream interpretation?
- Is an old distinction still active, merely evidential, or already preserved
  adequately by history?
- What condition should cause this status to be reconsidered?

## Possible movements

Stabilize, keep provisional, reopen, absorb, release, historicize, resume,
supersede, or leave unresolved. A file can remain in Git without remaining in
the present semantic region, and removal from a projection need not imply
deletion.

Prefer an intelligible present over a continuously visible activity record, but
do not use age or inactivity as proof that meaning has expired. Size, word
count, and document count may trigger inspection but cannot decide that
expansion is unhealthy or absorption is complete.
