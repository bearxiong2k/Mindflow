# Structuralist

Structuralist helps a project understand and transform its natural-language
system. Its five patterns sharpen judgment without prescribing a document tree
or turning the future into Mindflow state.

Let new or reforming meaning expand where its uncertainty can be understood.
Use the highest relevant interpretive position for the consequence at issue,
not automatically the broadest owner. As consequences settle, absorb them into
their current owners and formal realizations, then release provisional residue
after checking that necessary meaning survived. Evidence may reopen governing
meaning through explicit reform. Durable insight may remain interpretive, peer
perspectives may remain plural, and exact corrections may stay local.

## Locate the system within the project

Resolve the target root, read local instructions and Git state, and establish
what the project produces or operates and who owns the affected meaning.
Inspect enough of the present system and its dependencies to understand the
task; do not infer the whole from filenames or a search sample.

Distinguish the language through which the project understands, directs, and
evolves itself from the artifacts it produces. A template, manual, or other
textual deliverable does not become project authority merely because it is
rich in language. Recognize genuine dual roles explicitly. If no distinct
project document system exists, preserve that finding; construct one only when
authorized.

## Select only relevant patterns

- Read [distinction](references/patterns/distinction.md) when meanings may need
  to separate, combine, or become clearer.
- Read [position and relation](references/patterns/position-and-relation.md)
  when boundaries, placement, authority, dependency, navigation, or feedback
  matter.
- Read [perspective](references/patterns/perspective.md) when the same subject
  needs different questions, audiences, or levels.
- Read [temporality](references/patterns/temporality.md) when current,
  developing, settled, provisional, unresolved, or historical meanings differ.
- Read [transformation](references/patterns/transformation.md) when choosing
  an expansion, absorption, reconciliation, migration, refactor, or trim.

Compose patterns when needed. For structural overload, start with distinction,
position and relation, and transformation. Overload means responsibilities need
independent authority or change surfaces; length, age, density, or departure
from the recipe alone never justifies a split or trim.

Use [practice-realization.md](references/practice-realization.md) for exact

load a pattern only when a substantive judgment needs it.

Use [recipe.md](references/recipe.md) when a concrete comparison would help

Inspect `assets/recipe/` only for requested materialization or adaptation. The

nor permission to create files.

## Keep the work locally grounded

- Follow the project's authority and preserve pre-existing work. The skill,
  recipe, conversation, and generated views do not govern the project.
- Explicit invocation selects a method, not mutation permission. Within an
  authorized scope, make ordinary semantics-preserving repairs directly. Seek
  the proper authority when a choice would redirect the project, weaken a
  commitment, resolve protected uncertainty, or affect an out-of-scope artifact.
- After changes, check that consequences reached their owners, necessary
  evidence and uncertainty remain visible, and removed material had no unique
  obligation. Validate the Region when present, affected links, and the complete
  diff. For Structure changes, also perform the
  [navigation review](references/practice-realization.md#checking-navigable-relationships).
- Report unresolved choices and any incomplete validation. Recover only known
  invocation-owned edits; leave a reviewable diff when recovery is ambiguous.

Keep the invocation bounded to this request. Leave no watcher or private project
model. Do not infer authorization for migration, commits, pushes, or later work.
