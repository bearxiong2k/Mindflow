# Structuralist

Structuralist is Mindflow's future-facing Evolve methodology. It complements
Mindflow's authored historical record without turning judgment, execution, or
the future into SOE state.

Treat the project's natural-language layer as an evolving semantic system.
Shape it so its distinctions, positions, relations, perspectives, and
transformations remain coherent with the project without assuming its proper
form in advance.

Be precise about what to notice and deliberately open about what to build. The
patterns are lenses and possible movements, not a checklist, ontology, maturity
model, or required document tree.

Let genuinely new or reforming meaning expand in the highest relevant
interpretive position that can hold its uncertainty. As it stabilizes, absorb
its consequences into progressively more exact owners and formal realizations,
then release provisional residue. Evidence may pressure or reopen upstream
meaning but does not silently redefine its authority.

Treat this as a project-local partial order of realization, not a total file
hierarchy, fixed workflow, or demand for maximum formalization. Durable insight
may remain high-level, peer perspectives may stay plural, and an exact local
change need not begin in the project's broadest owner.

Maintaining this tension is a first-class responsibility. Notice premature
closure, settled consequences stranded in interpretive owners, drift between
governing meaning and realization, structurally overloaded owners, and residue
whose work is complete. An owner is overloaded only when its meanings need
independent authority or change surfaces—not because the file is long, dense,
or unlike a suggested structure. Resolve the pressure proportionately; do not
default to splitting or turn every invocation into a whole-system audit.

## Locate the system within the project

Orient to the project before analyzing its natural-language system. Reach a
proportionate account of what the project is, what it produces or operates, how
work changes it, and where relevant authority is carried. Use implementation,
manifests, tests, history, and natural language as evidence when useful; do not
begin from a Markdown inventory.

Identify the natural-language system by project function, not textual form. It
consists of the carriers through which the project understands, directs,
constrains, or evolves itself. A natural-language artifact may be semantically
rich while remaining an artifact of the project that produces it. A carrier
may serve both functions, but treat that overlap as a finding, not a default.

Only after forming a working account of this boundary should you select
patterns; use them to test or transform it. Do not project a Mindflow Region
until the boundary is clear enough for the task. If no distinct project system
exists, keep that absence visible rather than reclassifying artifacts to fill
it.

## Select only relevant patterns

- Read [patterns/distinction.md](references/patterns/distinction.md) when
  meanings may need to separate, combine, become clearer, or differ in
  settlement or realization.
- Read
  [patterns/position-and-relation.md](references/patterns/position-and-relation.md)
  when system boundary, placement, authority, realization direction, feedback,
  dependency, navigation, or coupling matters.
- Read [patterns/perspective.md](references/patterns/perspective.md) when the
  same subject is viewed through different questions, audiences, or levels.
- Read [patterns/temporality.md](references/patterns/temporality.md) when
  current, developing, expanding, settled, absorbed, provisional, evidential,
  unresolved, or historical meaning is changing status.
- Read [patterns/transformation.md](references/patterns/transformation.md) when
  creating, expanding, diagnosing, formalizing, migrating, refactoring,
  absorbing, trimming, or retiring structure.

Load several patterns when the situation crosses them. Do not load one merely
because its vocabulary resembles an existing filename.

For a direct structural-overload question, begin with distinction, position and
relation, and transformation. Add perspective or temporality only when
independent reader treatments or the difference between temporary expansion
and settled responsibility materially affects the judgment.

## Realize the practice when needed

Read [practice-realization.md](references/practice-realization.md) when using or
interpreting Mindflow, proposing a concrete document structure, or using the
current Structuralist glossary. It is a thin, revisable realization of the
patterns, not their source or a required local vocabulary.

Read [recipe.md](references/recipe.md) when a concrete suggested structure
would help understand, compare, construct, maintain, migrate, or refactor the
target at any lifecycle phase. Treat the recipe as an optional reference, not a
shape to adopt.

Also route a direct named or functional request through this compact match:

| User asks for | Recipe focus |
| --- | --- |
| Agent-facing repository guidance | `AGENTS.md` |
| Document-system governance | `governance/Documentation.md` |
| Mindflow Region topology or SOE semantics | Ordinary filesystem entries plus `MINDFLOW.yaml`; Mermaid views are generated |
| A durable perspective | `profiles/` |
| Current implementation-facing meaning | `canonical_docs/` |
| Developing direction or Opening | `long_term_steering/` |
| A protected unresolved choice | `deferred_questions/` |

| Reusable maintenance guidance | `trim_manual/` |

Match by responsibility, not literal wording. A direct match loads the recipe
and focuses the work on that responsibility; it does not justify adopting the
rest of the recipe or inspecting its assets.

Inspect `assets/recipe/` only when the requested work calls for materializing or
adapting reusable files. Reading the recipe does not imply asset use.

## Keep the work locally grounded

- Resolve the target project and follow its instructions, authority, scope, and
  permissions. Preserve pre-existing work.
- After locating the project-relative boundary, read enough of the present
  system and its surroundings to understand the forces relevant to the task.
  Do not infer the whole from filenames, metadata, or a search sample.
- Treat ordinary project files as local meaning according to their role.
  Artifact meaning does not become project-system authority by implication;
  neither do Structuralist, its patterns, its suggested glossary,
  conversations, or Mindflow views.
- Explicit invocation selects this skill but does not authorize mutation. A
  request to analyze, advise, or diagnose remains read-only unless change is
  also authorized.
- Interpret the authority available in the task rather than assuming its
  source. Human direction may be decisive when present; project rules,
  implementation, evidence, history, audiences, and lifecycle pressure may be
  decisive elsewhere.
- Maintain the natural-language side of the formalization gradient without
  claiming development direction or formal-artifact mutation that the task has
  not authorized.
- After an authorized change, validate the resulting Mindflow Region when
  present, review the complete diff, and report unresolved semantic choices.
  When constructing or reforming Structure, also review the root and affected
  folder scopes for consequential relationships using the
  [navigation review](references/practice-realization.md#checking-navigable-relationships).
  Successful validation or address resolution alone does not establish that
  the relevant authority, evidence, and realization connections are legible.

Leave no watcher, private project model, automatic migration, or permission for
future work. Stop rather than manufacture certainty when the target, authority,
scope, or meaning required for a consequential change remains ambiguous.
