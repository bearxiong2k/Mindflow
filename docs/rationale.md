# Why Mindflow?

A long-running project accumulates purpose, constraints, explanations, open
questions, and decisions as people and agents work on it. Files and Git keep
that material available, but availability alone does not make the maintained
whole easy to understand.

Mindflow gives the project a small, explicit account of that open meaning.
It keeps the account in ordinary files and one manifest so a person can return,
find the governing language, and steer work without depending on a previous
agent session.

## Keep the maintained whole bounded

Human attention remains limited even as agents produce more artifacts.
Selecting a useful Region makes responsibility visible. It is not an attempt
to ingest the entire repository or retain every discussion. When the account
becomes difficult to grasp, clarify owners and absorb settled meaning rather
than automatically adding more storage or organization machinery.

## Let existing organization carry its weight

Folders already supply placement and context. Documents already contain
headings, links, and exact language. Git already records byte history.
Mindflow derives those physical facts and adds stable identity, deliberate
selection, sparse relationships, and SOE where they are useful. It avoids
maintaining another authored copy of the file tree.

## Preserve judgment

Structure says what is maintained now. Opening says what is explicitly open.
Evolve records selected meaningful transformation and evidence. None decides
that a claim is true, a task is complete, or an artifact has become authoritative.
Those judgments remain with the project and its responsible participants.

Formalized behavior and open interpretation can live in the same artifact.
Tests may pressure a design, and design may change tests, without either
silently becoming the other's owner. The optional Structuralist methodology
helps an agent carry that conversation through ordinary reviewed edits.

## Keep the tools replaceable

GUI and CLI expose the same account for different reading needs. Removing a
view, cache, or installed skill must leave accepted project meaning recoverable
from its files, manifest, and Git. This is the practical test behind Mindflow's
small core and explicit boundaries.

These are design commitments, not claims of universally measured productivity
or deterministic agent behavior. See [semantics](semantics.md) and try the
[repository tour](tutorial/README.md) to evaluate them directly.
